# CFD Study and Comparative Corrosion Analysis of Pipeline Materials

**Personal Engineering Project | September 2026**

## Project Overview

This project investigates the behavior of industrial pipeline materials exposed to water flow through multiphysics numerical simulation using COMSOL Multiphysics 6.3.

The study compares three materials: **AISI 316L stainless steel, AISI 304L stainless steel, and carbon steel**, with a focus on corrosion behavior and the influence of fluid–structure interactions.

## Objectives

- Simulate turbulent water flow inside an industrial pipeline.
- Investigate the mechanical response of the pipeline under operating conditions.
- Study electrochemical corrosion behavior.
- Compare the suitability of three pipeline materials for industrial water applications.

## Methodology

**1. Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)**

- Turbulent flow simulation using the SST turbulence model.
- Analysis of flow behavior and its interaction with the pipeline.

**2. Structural Mechanics**

- Evaluation of Von Mises stress distribution.
- Assessment of pipeline deformation under fluid loading.
- Fluid–Structure Interaction (FSI) analysis.

**3. Electrochemical Corrosion**

- Electrochemical modeling using Secondary Current Distribution.
- Investigation of metal dissolution.
- Comparative analysis of 316L, 304L and carbon steel.

## Key Simulation Results

- **Maximum Von Mises stress:** approximately 0.439 MPa.
- **Maximum displacement:** approximately 0.0457 mm.
- Comparative evaluation of corrosion susceptibility in chloride-containing water.
